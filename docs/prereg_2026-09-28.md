# Pre-registration — follow-up experiments (2026-09-28)

Written and committed **before** any of the runs below produced data. The
only results known at writing time are the legacy MC16 sweep (layer 11,
orth + support-avoid) and exploratory feature correlations on it (null).

## Background facts fixed at writing time

- "late" = output of GPT-2 block 11, the last block. An edit there reaches the
  logits only through `ln_f` + unembedding, at the same position. There is no
  downstream computation, so the J-lens at layer 11 equals the logit lens.
- The layer-11 source direction was learned on `hidden_states[12]`, which is
  **post-`ln_f`**, but applied pre-`ln_f`. At layers 1 and 6 (`early`, `mid`)
  the learned directions (`grid_learn_s1337_{early,mid}`, 30% holdout, seed
  1337) are learned and applied in the same space.

## Design

Three controls per seed (1–16): `orthogonal_random`,
`orthogonal_random_support_avoid_top512`, `orthogonal_random_random_mask_top512`,
each relative to the layer's own source direction. Effect = block-bootstrap
mean `abs_red` on the fixed 5% held-out blocks (holdout seed 1337), 5,000
resamples. Edit: h ← h − α(h·d)d on non-name tokens.

- **Matched cost (MC):** α calibrated per direction to a common per-seed PLL
  cost target (layer 11: legacy targets pinned; layers 1 and 6: auto target =
  5% of the weakest control's cost at α = 40).
- **Unit projection (P1):** α = 1 (a true projection), no calibration.

## Hypotheses and decision rules

**H1 — SE calibration.** Each of 9 fixed directions (seeds 1–3 × 3 controls,
layer 11, matched α) is re-evaluated on 16 held-out splits (holdout seeds
5000–5015). Pass if the pooled Q/df lies inside the simulated 95% band for a
calibrated χ². If it lies above, all heterogeneity Q values below are divided
by the pooled Q/df before testing, and the paper says so.

**H2 — heterogeneity at matched cost, per layer.** For each layer ∈ {1, 6, 11}
and control, Cochran's Q across the 16 directions (after the H1 correction if
needed). Prediction: significant at p < 0.05/9 for all 9 cells. Reported
either way; a layer where it fails means the finding is layer-specific.

**H3 — matched norm vs matched cost.** For each layer and control, compare the
between-direction SD τ under P1 vs MC. Prediction (following the J-space
paper's matched-norm result): at layers 1 and 6, Q under P1 is not significant
(p ≥ 0.05) for a majority of the 6 cells, and τ(P1) < τ(MC). No prediction
for layer 11 under P1.

**H4 — J-space propagation (layers 1 and 6 only).** Fit a Jacobian lens for
GPT-2 (anthropics/jacobian-lens, WikiText-103 test split, 128-token
sequences). For each direction v, A(v) = ‖J_ℓ v‖ (unit v). Prediction:
Spearman ρ(A(v), |effect under P1|) > 0, one-sided p < 0.05, pooled across
the 48 directions of each layer. Secondary (exploratory): the same under MC,
where α calibration may absorb A(v).

**H5 — second model.** Replicate whichever of H2/H3 hold on Qwen3-0.6B at
the analogous early/mid/late layers. Details fixed before that run in an
addendum to this file.

## Addendum B — KL-matched design (written after the PLL-matched MC runs, before any KL-matched run)

**Why.** The pre-registered MC condition matched each seed to its own auto target (5% of the
weakest control's PLL cost at α = 40). Two flaws surfaced in the GPT-2 block 1 and 6 runs:
(i) on 3/16 seeds per layer the weakest control's cost at α = 40 was ≈ 0 (the PLL cost counts
only log-prob *drops*), so the target fell below the 0.003 tolerance and α = 0 "matched" —
a no-op with a zero-width CI; (ii) targets differ across seeds (≈ 10–60×), so across-direction
heterogeneity under MC partly reflects seed-level cost differences (|effect| vs target:
Spearman ρ = +0.43, p = 0.007 at block 1; +0.28, p = 0.054 at block 11). Those MC results are
reported as run, with both flaws disclosed, as secondary evidence. The Qwen MC run was stopped
after 4 seeds for the same reason.

**New condition (KL).** Cost = mean next-token KL(original ‖ edited) over every position of
the first 100 WikiText-103 test paragraphs (≤ 64 tokens; corpus sha256 `7e0015e0…`), with the
edit applied at all positions. This follows the KL-to-original criterion used by heretic
(p-e-w/heretic @ `3521f86`, which scores the first generated token only). The corpus is
disjoint from the bias sentences, and KL is zero only for a no-op edit.

**Target.** One fixed target for every model, layer, seed and control: **KL = 0.10 nats per
position**, tolerance ±0.005 (5%). Chosen from a cost-only pilot on seeds 101–102
(`scripts/pilot_kl_cost.py`, `runs/pilot_kl_{gpt2,qwen}.json`): every pilot direction at every
layer of both models exceeds 0.21 at α = 20. α range [0, 100]. A direction that cannot be
matched is reported as unmatched; α = 0 is never a valid match (target > tolerance).

**Scope.** GPT-2 blocks 1, 6, 11 and Qwen3-0.6B blocks 1, 14, 26; seeds 1–16; the same three
controls; same effect metric, split and statistics.

**Predictions (replacing the MC parts of H2/H3/H5).**
- H2-KL: heterogeneity (Cochran's Q) significant at p < 0.05/18 in every one of the 18 cells.
- H3-KL: τ(KL) > τ(P1) in a majority of the 18 cells (a stronger, cost-matched edit spreads
  the random-direction effects more than a unit projection).
